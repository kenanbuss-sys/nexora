# Domain Spec — CSM (Advanced Scope)

## Purpose
Customer support/case management across channels, complaints, SLA, knowledge and customer satisfaction. Owns Case, CaseMessage/Interaction, CaseSLA and KnowledgeArticle publication workflow. Does not replace product ServiceOrder; support cases link to service/RMA/order as needed.

## Status
P2/P3 architecture-reserved. Full feature spec and ownership refinement required before implementation.

## Constraints
Tenant isolation, permissions, audit, canonical IDs, event/adapter boundaries, versioned policy and no conflict with CRM/SVC/DOC/BI ownership.
