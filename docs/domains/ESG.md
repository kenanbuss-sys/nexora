# Domain Spec — ESG (Advanced Scope)

## Purpose
Optional environmental/sustainability data and KPI capabilities. Architecture supports measurements, supplier/product attributes, targets and reporting without forcing one jurisdiction-specific ESG standard into the core.

## Status
P2/P3 architecture-reserved. Full feature spec and ownership refinement required before implementation.

## Constraints
Tenant isolation, permissions, audit, canonical IDs, event/adapter boundaries, versioned policy and no conflict with CRM/SVC/DOC/BI ownership.
