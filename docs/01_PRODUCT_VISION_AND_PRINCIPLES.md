# Product Vision & Principles

A configurable enterprise Business Operating System that replaces fragmented spreadsheets, paper and disconnected operational tools while integrating systems that should remain.

It must support B2B/B2C selling, POS, OMS, procurement/import, WMS, configurable and standard manufacturing, MRP/APS, quality/traceability, maintenance/service/field work, projects/job costing, workforce operations, management finance, analytics/AI, scanners/printers/RFID/scales/kiosks/machines/IoT and existing accounting/commerce/banking/courier/industrial systems.

**Capture once -> validate once -> use everywhere -> trace end to end.**

Principles:
1. Business process first.
2. One operational truth per concept.
3. Configurable, not forked.
4. End-to-end digital thread.
5. Physical-world verification as a platform capability.
6. Progressive integration/migration.
7. Enterprise permissions, approvals and audit.
8. Exception-driven operations.
9. Provider neutrality.
10. Evolution through extensions rather than rewrites.

Commercial packs are configuration/licensing bundles, not code forks: Platform Core, Commerce, Distribution/WMS, Manufacturing, Advanced Manufacturing, Service/Field, Finance/Management Control, Governance, Intelligence/AI.
