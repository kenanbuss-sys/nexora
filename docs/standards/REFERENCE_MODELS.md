# Reference Models & Standards

Use as capability/architecture references, never copied implementation: ISA-95; ISA-88 when batch/process applies; GS1 identifiers/traceability (GTIN, SSCC, lots/serials); SCOR Plan/Source/Make/Deliver/Return/Enable; BPMN process concepts; DMN decision concepts; OPC UA industrial interoperability; ISO 9001 quality concepts; ISO/IEC 27001 information-security management; OWASP ASVS/application-security guidance; NIST cybersecurity/risk concepts where appropriate; ITIL service concepts where useful.

Leading enterprise suites may be used for capability benchmarking only; product design remains original and vendor-neutral.
